youtube-clipper

AI Video Pipeline & Side Business

A Python CLI and local web GUI that turns long-form video into short-form vertical clips, each shipping with per-platform upload copy for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and X. The pipeline runs yt-dlp or a local source, faster-whisper for word-level transcription, Claude for segment ranking and copywriting, then ffmpeg and mediapipe for face-tracked reframing, karaoke captions and overlays. I run the themed accounts it publishes to, so the distribution side is mine as well as the tooling.
